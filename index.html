Lei Geral de Proteção de Dados

Law No. 13,709 of August 14, 2018

"This Law provides on the processing of personal data, including by digital means, by a natural person or legal entity governed by public or private law, for the purpose of protecting the essential rights of freedom and privacy."

Max Penalty
R$ 50M

Per infraction (or 2% of revenue) for data breaches.

Your Rights
Control

Access, correct, anonymize, port, or delete your data.

Consent
Free

Must be informed, unequivocal, and for a specific purpose.

Regulator
ANPD

National Data Protection Authority enforces the law.
